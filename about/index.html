About

Engineering expertise meets software development.

Hi, I'm Adalberto

I'm a mechanical engineer with deep experience in the pump industry — from design and drafting to 3D modeling and production workflows. Over the years, I saw how much time engineers spent on repetitive tasks that software could handle in seconds.

That realization led me into software development. I taught myself iOS development with SwiftUI and dove into CAD API programming, combining my engineering domain knowledge with code to build tools that actually solve real problems.

Today, I work at the intersection of both worlds: automating engineering workflows through custom CAD tools and building privacy-first iOS apps. The combination of understanding the engineering problem and being able to write the software is what sets my work apart.
