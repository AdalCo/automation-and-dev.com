Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 4, 2026

  • Your data stays on your device and in your private iCloud account
  • We do not collect, store, or have access to any of your data
  • We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs
  • The App is built entirely with Apple first-party frameworks

Automation & Development ("we," "us," or "our") built the Track$mart app ("the App") as a personal budgeting tool. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

What Data the App Stores

The App allows you to create and manage the following data locally on your device:

  • Transactions (amounts, descriptions, dates, merchants)
  • Accounts (names, types, balances)
  • Budgets (spending limits, categories, time periods)
  • Categories and tags
  • Savings goals
  • Recurring transactions
  • Categorization and account rules
  • App preferences and settings

All of this data is entered by you manually. The App does not automatically collect data from your bank, email, or other sources unless you explicitly enable bank sync through Apple's FinanceKit framework.

How Your Data Is Stored

  • On-device: All data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework
  • iCloud sync: If you have iCloud enabled, your data is synced across your devices using Apple's CloudKit. This data is stored in your private iCloud account and is not accessible to us or any third party
  • No servers: We do not operate any servers. Your data never passes through our infrastructure

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party services, SDKs, or frameworks. Everything is built using Apple first-party technologies:

  • SwiftData (local storage)
  • CloudKit (iCloud sync)
  • StoreKit 2 (in-app subscriptions, processed by Apple)
  • FinanceKit (optional bank sync, processed by Apple)
  • UserNotifications (local notifications only)

Biometric Authentication

The App may use Face ID or Touch ID to protect access to your data. Biometric authentication is handled entirely by Apple's LocalAuthentication framework. We do not access, store, or process any biometric data.

Notifications

The App uses local notifications only (e.g., budget alerts, savings goal reminders). These notifications are generated on your device. We do not send push notifications from a remote server.

In-App Purchases

Subscription purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect or have access to your payment information, Apple ID, or billing details.

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or disclose any of your data to third parties. Period.

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored on your device and in your private iCloud account, you have full control:

  • Delete app data: Delete the App from your device to remove all local data
  • Delete iCloud data: Go to Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Storage → Track$mart → Delete Data
  • Delete specific items: Use the App's built-in delete functions for individual transactions, accounts, budgets, etc.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy
