Automation & Development ("we," "us," or "our") built the Track$mart app ("the App") as a personal budgeting tool. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

What Data the App Stores

The App allows you to create and manage the following data locally on your device:

  • Transactions (amounts, descriptions, dates, merchants)
  • Accounts (names, types, balances)
  • Budgets (spending limits, categories, time periods)
  • Categories and tags
  • Savings goals
  • Recurring transactions
  • Categorization and account rules
  • App preferences and settings

All of this data is entered by you manually. The App does not automatically collect data from your bank, email, or other sources.

Technologies Used

The App is built entirely using Apple first-party frameworks. No third-party services, SDKs, or libraries are integrated:

  • SwiftData (local storage)
  • CloudKit (iCloud sync)
  • StoreKit 2 (in-app subscriptions, processed entirely by Apple)
  • UserNotifications (local notifications only, no remote push)
  • LocalAuthentication (Face ID / Touch ID — we never access or store biometric data)

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or disclose any of your data to third parties. Period.

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored on your device and in your private iCloud account, you have full control:

  • Delete app data: Delete the App from your device to remove all local data
  • Delete iCloud data: Go to Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Storage → Track$mart → Delete Data
  • Delete specific items: Use the App's built-in delete functions for individual transactions, accounts, budgets, etc.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
